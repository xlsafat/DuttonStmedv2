Care Outside Regular Hours

After Hours

Our After Hours service gives you access to care outside our regular clinic hours, so you're never without support when you need it. Whether it's a health concern that can't wait until our next opening, our After Hours team is here to help.

Service Hours

When you can reach us

Our After Hours service operates on the schedule below, extending well beyond our standard reception hours so care is available in the evenings and on weekends.

  • Extended weekday availability, evenings included
  • Weekend coverage every Saturday and Sunday
  • Bulk billed for all patients with a valid Medicare card

For life-threatening emergencies, always call 000 or go to your nearest Emergency Department.
