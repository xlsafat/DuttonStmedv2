Our Services

A comprehensive range of bulk billed care, delivered by an experienced team, for every stage of life.

Iron Infusion

In-clinic iron infusions for patients with diagnosed iron deficiency, delivered safely under the supervision of our GPs.

Bulk billing available with a valid Medicare card.

General Health

We offer thorough health checks for women, men, children, seniors, and sexual health, ensuring holistic care for every stage of life.

Mental Health

Our experienced team provides compassionate counselling and mental health management, supporting emotional wellbeing and resilience.

Minor Procedures

We perform a range of minor surgical procedures, including cryosurgery and skin checks, right here in our clinic.

Vaccinations

Stay protected with our full range of immunisations and seasonal flu shots, administered in a safe and supportive environment.

Pregnancy Care

We support your journey with antenatal care and contraceptive services, including Implanon insertion and removal.

Chronic Disease

Our team helps you manage long-term conditions such as diabetes and hypertension, with a focus on lifestyle and weight management.

Workplace Injuries

We accept WorkCover and CTP claims, offering timely assessment, treatment, and return-to-work planning.

Preventive Health

We focus on early detection, screening, and lifestyle advice to help you stay ahead of potential health issues.

Immunization

A full childhood and adult immunisation schedule delivered under the National Immunisation Program, tracked and recorded on the Australian Immunisation Register.

Telehealth

Phone consultations are available for eligible patients who can’t make it in person — just ask our reception team when you book.

Specialist Services

Access to specialist care across the following areas.

Gastroenterology

Diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the digestive system.

Internal Medicine

Specialist care for complex or chronic adult medical conditions.

Paediatrics

Specialist medical care for infants, children, and adolescents.

Allied Health Services

Complementary care to support your recovery and wellbeing.

Exercise Physiology

Exercise-based programs to help manage chronic conditions and support recovery.

Dietitian

Personalised nutrition advice and dietary management to support your health goals.

Psychology

Confidential counselling and support for mental health and wellbeing.

Physiotherapy

Treatment to restore movement and function after injury, illness, or surgery.

Podiatry

Assessment and treatment of foot, ankle, and lower limb conditions.
